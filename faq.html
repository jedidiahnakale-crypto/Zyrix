Frequently asked questions

What is Zyrix?

A free app that shows, with cartoons, how real attacks and scams happen and how to stop them. After each case you pick the fix and take a short quiz.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no ads and nothing to pay.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Sign up with your name, email and a password of at least 8 characters, then log in.

Is my password safe?

Your password is scrambled before it is saved, so nobody can read it. Use a password you do not use anywhere else.

Who are Zy and Nia?

Zy is the chat robot who answers security questions. Nia is the robot who explains things during the cartoons.

Are the people and companies real?

No. All people, companies and data in the cases are made up. No real system is touched.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. In Chrome on Android, tap Install app or "Add to Home screen". On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".

Does it work without internet?

Open it once with internet so it saves a copy. After that the cases, cartoons and quizzes work offline. Logging in, voice and the owner console need internet.

How do I use my voice?

Press the mic button in the Zy chat and speak. It works best in Chrome or Edge.

What does Zy do if he does not know?

He says so honestly and does not guess. Please do not type secrets into Zy, because questions he cannot answer are saved.

I forgot my password.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email jedidiahnakale@gmail.com and ask. See the Privacy Policy.
