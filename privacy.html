Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Zyrix is a free learning app about cyber safety. It is run by Jedidiah Nakale in Namibia. This page says, in plain words, what we save about you.

What we save

  • Your name and email, when you sign up.
  • Your password. It is scrambled before it is saved, so nobody can read it, not even us.
  • What you do in the app: which cases you open, your quiz scores, and the questions Zy could not answer (up to 200 letters each). These are linked to your account.
  • On your own device: your login, your settings (like music or slow speech) and your progress. This stays in your browser.

What we do not do

  • No ads. We do not sell or share your data.
  • No tracking cookies and no third-party analytics.

Please do not type secrets into Zy

Questions Zy cannot answer are saved so we can improve him. Never type passwords, ID numbers or private company information into Zy.

Voice

The microphone is optional. If you use it, your browser's speech service turns your voice into text (in Chrome, the sound goes to Google). We never save your voice.

Who can see your data

Only the owner of Zyrix can see the list of users and the usage numbers, through a private console. Your account data is stored with Supabase, our login and database service. Its servers may be outside Namibia.

Fonts

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google may see your internet address when the page loads.

Your choices

You can ask to see or delete your account and everything linked to it. Email the owner, Jedidiah Nakale, at jedidiahnakale@gmail.com. Zyrix is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too.
